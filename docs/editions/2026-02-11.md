### c) Foodborne/waterborne (Salmonella, *E. coli*, hepatitis A)

**What happened:** CDC expanded a multistate investigation of **Salmonella linked to dietary supplements containing moringa leaf powder**. As of **Jan 29**, CDC reported **65 illnesses across 28 states**, added an additional Salmonella strain, and updated recall information. *(Ref pending)*

**Where:** Multistate exposure via nationally distributed supplement products (potential California spillover via retail/online purchase). *(Ref pending)*

**Who is affected:** Higher risk for severe illness in young children, older adults, and immunocompromised people (CDC reiteration). *(Ref pending)*

**What we know vs. don’t know:**
- **Known:** Product category/vehicle (moringa-containing supplements), recalls, and case count reported by CDC as of Jan 29. *(Ref pending)*
- **Unknown:** California-specific case counts were not enumerated in CDC’s media alert; local burden may lag due to reporting and subtyping timelines. *(Ref pending)*

**Practical implications:**
- **Clinicians/labs:** Consider targeted exposure history for diarrheal illness (including supplement use); ensure specimens are cultured/subtyped when indicated. *(Ref pending)*
- **Retail/occupational:** Reinforce recall execution and sanitation of surfaces potentially contaminated by supplement powders/capsules. *(Ref pending)*
